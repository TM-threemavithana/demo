ABOUT US
Ceylon Spice Company is a Sri Lankan specialty-spice business that supplies carefully selected whole spices, ground spices and ready-to-use blends. Our range is designed for home cooks, food enthusiasts, cafés, restaurants and small food businesses seeking authentic Sri Lankan flavour.

Our catalogue includes Ceylon cinnamon, black pepper, cardamom, cloves, nutmeg, mace, coriander, cumin and fennel, as well as ground spices and signature blends. Product availability, package sizes and current prices can change; customers should ask the assistant to check the live catalogue before placing an order.

PRODUCT QUALITY AND STORAGE
- Products are packed in food-grade, sealed packaging.
- Keep spices in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture.
- Close the package tightly after use to preserve aroma and freshness.
- Whole spices usually retain their flavour longer than ground spices. Grind whole spices shortly before cooking for best results.
- Products contain only the ingredients stated on their label. Customers with allergies should read the label and contact us before ordering if they need ingredient clarification.

ORDERING
- Customers may place an order through WhatsApp, our website chat, or by contacting customer care.
- Please provide the product name, package size where applicable, quantity, delivery address, contact number and preferred payment method.
- The final order value, delivery charge and availability are confirmed before dispatch.
- Large or wholesale orders should be requested at least three working days in advance, subject to stock availability.
- An order is considered confirmed only after the customer receives an order confirmation from the Ceylon Spice Company team.

DELIVERY
- We deliver island-wide within Sri Lanka through selected courier partners.
- Colombo and nearby suburbs: normally delivered within 1-2 working days after dispatch.
- Other areas in Sri Lanka: normally delivered within 2-4 working days after dispatch.
- Remote-area delivery may take longer depending on the courier route and weather conditions.
- Delivery charges depend on location, parcel weight and order value. The exact charge is confirmed before an order is finalised.
- Same-day delivery is not guaranteed and is available only for selected Colombo-area orders when confirmed by our team.
- Customers should provide a complete delivery address, a reachable phone number and any useful delivery instructions.

PAYMENT OPTIONS
- Cash on delivery may be available for eligible Sri Lankan orders.
- Bank transfer is accepted for confirmed orders; payment details are provided during order confirmation.
- Online payment options may be offered for selected orders.
- Wholesale and custom orders may require an advance payment.
- Please do not send payment until the order details and total have been confirmed by our team.

RETURNS, REPLACEMENTS AND CANCELLATIONS
- If an item arrives damaged, incorrect or incomplete, customers should contact us within 48 hours of delivery with the order reference and clear photos where possible.
- Eligible issues will be reviewed for replacement, credit or another suitable resolution.
- For food-safety reasons, opened products cannot normally be returned unless there is a verified quality issue.
- Order cancellations are accepted before dispatch. Once an order has been handed to the courier, cancellation may not be possible.
- Refund timing, where applicable, depends on the original payment method and review outcome.
